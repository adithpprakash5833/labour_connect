OUR PURPOSE

Local skills.
Stronger communities.

Labour Connect helps people find reliable local work and the professionals to do it.

A better way to find a helping hand.

Finding an electrician, a painter, or an extra pair of hands often starts with a neighbour’s number or a message in a group chat. We bring that discovery into one clear, accessible place.

How trust works here

Workers submit professional details and private verification documents. An administrator reviews each profile before it appears in search or can accept work. Verification is a platform review, and customers should still discuss the scope of work before booking.

Reviews come from completed services. Rates, availability and work status stay visible so everyone can make informed decisions.

An MCA mini project with working workflows

This academic marketplace demonstrates role-based access, verification, local discovery, bookings, open jobs, payment recording and service reviews. Demo online payments simulate a successful record; they do not collect or transfer money.
